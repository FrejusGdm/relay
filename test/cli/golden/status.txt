Usage
  relay status [--job <id>] [--json]

Show the job, its workers, accounts and checkpoints.
relay status works without the background service; it then shows the saved state.

Examples
  relay status
  relay status --json
  relay status --job 3f9a2c1d

Options
      --job <id>           Show this job instead of the one in this folder
      --json               Print the status as JSON
  -h, --help               Show this help
      --log-level <level>  How much to log: debug, info, warn or error
