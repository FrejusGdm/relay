Usage
  relay account <list|add|status|login|remove> [<provider> <name> | <provider:name>]

Add, list, check, sign in to or remove accounts.
Each account has its own profile folder.
Signing in runs the provider's own login. relay never sees your password or token.

Examples
  relay account list
  relay account add codex work
  relay account login codex:work

Options
  -h, --help               Show this help
      --log-level <level>  How much to log: debug, info, warn or error
