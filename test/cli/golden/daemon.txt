Usage
  relay daemon <start|stop|restart|status|run>

Start, stop or check relay's background service.
The service keeps live job state and listens only on a private socket in your relay folder.
relay starts it by itself when a command needs it.

Examples
  relay daemon status

Options
      --force              With stop or restart: also stop the agents the service started
  -h, --help               Show this help
      --log-level <level>  How much to log: debug, info, warn or error
