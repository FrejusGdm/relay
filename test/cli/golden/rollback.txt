Usage
  relay rollback [<checkpoint>] [--yes] [--dry-run]

Return the files to an earlier checkpoint.
relay shows which files will change and saves your current files first, so you can undo it.
Your branch, commits and staged changes stay as they are.
Without a checkpoint, relay uses the newest one not saved before a rollback.

Examples
  relay rollback
  relay rollback 3 --dry-run
  relay rollback 3 --yes

Options
      --yes                Roll back without asking
      --dry-run            Show what would change, and change nothing
  -h, --help               Show this help
      --log-level <level>  How much to log: debug, info, warn or error
