Usage
  relay doctor --reindex

Rebuild relay's index of jobs.
relay rebuilds the index from the .relay/ files and git, so nothing about your jobs is lost.

Examples
  relay doctor --reindex

Options
      --reindex            Rebuild the index from the job files
  -h, --help               Show this help
      --log-level <level>  How much to log: debug, info, warn or error
